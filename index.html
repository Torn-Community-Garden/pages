Rules

  1. We are an 18+ faction.

  2. Register for TornStats.

  3. Take the “Intravenous Therapy” course (BIO2127) as soon as possible. (Read more under Expectations -> Armory -> Blood bags)

  4. No loss selling after Level 15.

  5. Do not cause harm to another member's in-game character unless consented beforehand. Banter/pranks are fine as long as both parties are in on it.

  6. You must be active - After 5 days (or multiple times of 3 days) of inactivity, you will be removed from the faction unless Council has been notified of your absence.

  7. Related to the rule above, if you will be away or know you won’t make it to a war, please report either to a council member directly or in the #absences channel on Discord.
    • It is completely fine to have rough patches and be unavailable; however, for both our members' and your own sake, speak to a council member about it. They are always willing to advise and work out the best path forward.

Expectations

Organized Crimes

  1. Follow our OC guidelines.

  2. OC Delays - Organized Crimes are a great way to personally earn money and help the faction. However, delays affect everyone on your team and ruin other members' chances of flying/making money, as well as their ability to plan when they can join another OC.
    • As such, delaying an OC will result in a fine being taken off your cut. The amount that is to be fined is decided by the council depending on how long the delay is, the reason for the delay, and how many times you have delayed in the past. Meaning, repeated delays will result in greater fines, and if too excessive, may result in faction removal.

Armory

  1. Gear - Weapon/Armor loaning is open to all.
    • However, please return any Warlord you have on you when you are not using it. This is to make sure that they are available for anyone who can make use of them at the time. Primarily for war. Be aware that holding onto one may result in it being retrieved without warning, so make sure you have a second weapon equipped so you don’t end up empty-handed.
    • If you see someone holding a Warlord weapon who is also offline/not using it, feel free to retrieve it so you may use it. (If you can not retrieve items, ask anyone who is a Rose+)

  2. Blood bags - To use blood bags, you need to take the Intravenous therapy course as stated in our requirements.
    • Whenever you have this, we’d like it if you stick to using Blood bags instead of other resources, as this saves the faction a lot of money. (People with type AB+ may still use ipecac, since they have no incompatible blood type.)

  3. Xanax - Available to all during chains/wars.
    • Training usage is a perk for members once they have achieved Dahlia rank. Therefore, loss selling or using Xanax for purposes other than to chain/war during those times is strictly prohibited. (This does not apply if the war you’ve actively participated in ends or the target score has been reached, and you still have E left. Feel free to spend that on training!)

  4. Temporary Items - Temps are unrestricted usage; however, please restrict checking out a maximum quantity of 30 smokes and 30 tears. As these are a bit more expensive.
    • Those who are holding more, we will manually retract the items, and you can check out the correct quantity again. This is to ensure there is enough to go around for everyone.

  5. Points Usage - Points usage will be restricted for wartime energy refill usage only.
    • This is to ensure members have every resource available for extra hits when it is critical for the faction's wars.
    • We do not use points for nerve bar refills.

War/Chains

  1. Chains and Wars - We have never been hard on anyone's case; however, we have always kept tabs in the background.
    • No war participation for two consecutive wars without reporting absence is going to result in a kick.
    • Check chat for hit calls, chain coordination, etc., so we inadvertently don’t steal hits from each other and can coordinate team hit efforts when necessary. Using both in-game and Discord chat would be ideal to make sure you don’t miss any information; however, in-game chat tends to be what we use more during war. Discord tends to be where we make most announcements, or if we otherwise need someone's attention, so it’s good to have pings on!

  2. Turtling - Just like the above, this is not the most strict expectation. But simply putting in an effort is enough. However, not turtling at all, therefore bleeding a lot of points, ultimately holds the faction back a lot. And if done excessively, it may result in measures being taken by us (we will always message and work with you before this ever becomes a reality).

  3. Bounties - We personally don’t particularly like using bounties, as it costs quite a lot of money if done excessively and aren’t very effective. So please only rely on them if absolutely necessary!
